# FlowDesk — LinkedIn launch post

Video: `docs/media/flowdesk-linkedin-1080x1350.mp4` (4:5, 1080×1350, 61 s, made to play muted).
Everything in the video is the real app running locally. The company,
"Northbeam Digital", and all its people and customers are fictional demo data.

---

Running a small business shouldn't take 9 browser tabs.

Tasks live in one tool. Invoices in another. Customer requests in someone's inbox. Who can see what? Nobody's quite sure.

So I built FlowDesk: one workspace for the whole operation.

→ Plan and assign work on a board or a calendar
→ Turn customer requests into tasks, then into invoices
→ Record payments and see what's paid and what's still owed
→ Analytics computed live from your real data, never estimated
→ 5 roles (owner, manager, employee, accountant, support), each enforced on the server, with data isolated per organization
→ An activity log in plain sentences, so every action is on the record

Under the hood: Flask + PostgreSQL + Redis on the backend, React + TypeScript on the front, and 1,250+ backend tests including cross-tenant security checks.

The demo company in the video, Northbeam Digital, is made up. The app isn't.
